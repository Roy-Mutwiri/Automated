## Priority when images disagree

1. Use 01 and 02 for the room layout.
2. Use 11 only as an approximate placement guide.
3. Use 07–10 and 20 for wall and ceiling design.
4. Use 13–19 for local equipment details.
5. Use 03–06 and 12 to check the result from other angles.

The discarded first drafts of images 01 and 06 are intentionally excluded. Build real separate 3D objects; do not project a complete reference image onto a wall and present it as a reconstructed room.
